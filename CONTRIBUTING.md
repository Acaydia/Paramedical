# Contributing to Paramedical

Thank you for helping improve this educational knowledge-base workspace. Contributions should prioritize clarity, source awareness, privacy, and safe use.

## Before you contribute

- Keep material **educational and general**; do not provide patient-specific medical advice.
- Remove patient-identifying, confidential, proprietary, or otherwise restricted information.
- Follow applicable licensure, training, scope-of-practice, organizational, and local requirements.
- Do not represent material as an official protocol, certification, endorsement, or clinical directive unless that status is documented and permission to share it is clear.

## Content checklist

For each new or substantially revised page:

1. Use a descriptive title and place it in a logical topic folder.
2. State the intended audience, context, or jurisdiction when that context affects interpretation.
3. Link or cite the source material and include its date or version when available.
4. Separate general educational information from any local protocol or policy.
5. Use precise, neutral language; avoid unsupported outcomes, guarantees, or efficacy claims.
6. Add a review date or note when content may change over time.
7. Check that examples are hypothetical or properly de-identified.

## Review expectations

Drafts should be reviewed by an appropriate maintainer or subject-matter reviewer before they are presented as ready for use. Review is not a substitute for users’ own responsibility to follow current authoritative guidance and local requirements.

## Suggested pull request notes

When proposing a change, briefly identify:

- the purpose of the addition or revision;
- the sources consulted;
- the intended audience or setting;
- any jurisdictional or protocol limitations; and
- any areas that still need review.
